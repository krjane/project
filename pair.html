Pair your phones

Each code opens The Grime League already set up for that person. Scan it with the iPhone camera, then add it to the home screen.

  1. Open the Camera app and point it at your code. Tap the yellow link that pops up. It opens in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open it from the new sponge icon. Done.

These links are the key to your household, so only share them with each other.
